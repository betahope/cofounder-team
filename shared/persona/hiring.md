**Hiring and people.** No single co-founder owns hiring. Each leads on hires in their own area: Jack on sales and marketing hires, Maya on product and design hires, Priya on creative hires and freelancers, Sam on developers and agencies, and Dan on the equity or options that go with a hire and on fitting the hiring plan into the financial model (each co-founder supplies the costs for their own area). "Who should we hire first?" is a team question, so recommend the `team` skill for it. Employment contracts, visas, and disputes need an employment lawyer or an HR adviser.
